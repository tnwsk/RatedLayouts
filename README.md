# Rated Maps

**A <cl>community-run & standalone</c> <cg>rating system</c> that focuses solely on <cl>Map Levels</c>!**

---

### About

This mod adds a <cg>new rating system</c> that focuses on the <cl>map design</c> and <cc>gameplay</c> rather than decoration and effects.

This gives <cy>map and gameplay creators</c> a platform to get their levels rated and recognized.

<cr>**This won't replace/conflict with existing rating systems nor will officially rated levels be included in this mod!**</c>

<co>**You can find the Rated Maps by clicking the Blue Spark button at the bottom left of the Creator Layer or Level Search Layer!**</c>

---

### Features

- **<cg>NEW</c> <cf>Map Rating System</c>**: Rates map levels based on map design and fun gameplay, rather than decoration and effects. This brings more creators who don't have the skill for decorated levels.
- **<cg>NEW</c> <cj>Sparks Collectables</c>**: Earn Sparks Collectables by completing classic rated map levels!
- **<cg>NEW</c> <co>Planets Collectables</c>**: Earn Planets Collectables by completing platformer rated map levels!
- **<cg>NEW</c> <cj>Blueprint Points</c>**: Earn Blueprint Points whenever you get a rated map level!
- **<cg>NEW</c> <cl>Blue Coins</c>**: Collect Blue Coins hidden in rated map levels.
- **<cg>NEW</c> <cr>Rubies</c>**: Earn Rubies and use them to unlock special cosmetics in the Ruby Shop!
- **<cg>Seamless Integration</c>**: Works alongside the existing rating system; this won't replace the current rating system but rather provides an alternative.
- **<cr>Improved Demon Ratings</c>**: More accurate demon ratings by giving harder demons more rewards.
- **<cg>Faster Rating Process</c>**: Community-focused rating system, which makes it easier to get levels rated quickly.
- **<co>Map Moderator/Admin Badges</c>**: Special badges for moderators to identify the map moderators and admins!
- **<cy>Leaderboard Integration</c>**: Compete with other players on leaderboards for the most Sparks, Planets and Creator Points!
- **<ca>Event Rated Map Levels</c>**: New daily, weekly and monthly rated map levels to showcase!
- **<co>Custom Search Filters</c>**: Easily search for rated map levels using various filters such as difficulty, level type, and more!
- **<cc>Community Voting System</c>**: Players can vote on suggested/rated maps based on originality, difficulty, and gameplay.
- **<cj>Map Gauntlets</c>**: Special gauntlets featuring themed classic rated map levels created by map/gameplay creators through creator contests.
- **<cf>The Spire</c>**: Platformer-focused, user-created maps levels. Explore the Spire and find forsaken lore beyond the <cp>Cosmos</c>.
- **<cr>Report System</c>**: Report rated maps that violate the Rated Maps guidelines. Help us maintain the quality of rated maps!
- **<cg>Custom Achievements</c>**: Custom Achievements for completing rated map levels and achieving milestones and other special interactions.
- **<cs>QOL Features!</c>**: Various quality of life improvements for both players and creators.
